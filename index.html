About

How I work.

Based inDarmstadt, Germany
RoleAI Consultant, Reply Leadvise
StudyingMSc CS, TU Darmstadt
FocusLLM systems & applied NLP
Working onHuacaya — secure on-prem LLM platform

I like the unglamorous half of machine learning — the part where a promising model has to survive real data, real latency budgets, and real people using it.

Most of my work has lived on that line. At Queryella I fine-tuned multi-label models and then had to make their output legible as an actual security report. At the Software Technology Group the model was never the hard part; getting clean, balanced training data was. At Reply the question is how you run LLMs at all when the data cannot leave the building.

I care about collaboration and clear communication as much as the code. I'd rather ask an obvious question early than build the wrong thing confidently, and I try to leave every codebase easier to read than I found it. Colleagues tend to describe me as approachable and steady under pressure — which mostly means I get curious instead of anxious when something breaks.

Right now I'm most interested in retrieval-augmented systems, evaluation that actually correlates with usefulness, and making AI tools that people trust because they can see how the answer was reached.
